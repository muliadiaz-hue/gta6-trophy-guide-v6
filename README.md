Leonida Platinum — the complete GTA 6 trophy guide and tracker for PlayStation 5: all 55 official trophies with their names, descriptions and grades, a cited guide for every one, a spoiler-safe checklist with PSN progress counters, a staged roadmap to platinum with a Next-up planner, and a missables page covering the one confirmed missable risk. Fully offline, no network at runtime, progress saved in the browser.
